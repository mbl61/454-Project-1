Image Normalization we implemented is as follows:
\begin{align*}
    Tmp(i,j,k) &= \frac{In(i,j,k)}{255}\\
    Out(i,j,k) &= \frac{Tmp(i,j,k) - \mu_k}{\sigma_k}
\end{align*}

And we want to turn it into shaped like:
\[
w \cdot In + b = Out
\]
, where $w$ and $b$ are the weights and bias of a convolutional layer.

We can rewrite the normalization equation as:
\begin{align*}
    Out(i,j,k) &= \frac{\frac{In(i,j,k)}{255} - \mu_k}{\sigma_k}\\
    Out(i,j,k) &= \frac{1}{\sigma_k}\left(\frac{In(i,j,k)}{255} - \mu_k\right)\\
    Out(i,j,k) &= \frac{1}{255 \cdot \sigma_k}In(i,j,k) - \frac{\mu_k}{\sigma_k}\\
\end{align*}

So we can see that the weights and bias of the convolutional layer are:
\begin{align*}
    w &= \frac{1}{255 \cdot \sigma_k}\\
    b &= -\frac{\mu_k}{\sigma_k}
\end{align*}

Then we put value in it:
\begin{align*}
    \mu = \begin{pmatrix}
        0.4914 & 0.4822 & 0.4465
    \end{pmatrix}\\
    \sigma = \begin{pmatrix}
        0.2470 & 0.2435 & 0.2616
    \end{pmatrix}
\end{align*}

The final weights and bias are:
\begin{align*}
    w &= \begin{pmatrix}
        0.0159 & 0.0161 & 0.0150
    \end{pmatrix}\\
    b &= \begin{pmatrix}
        -1.9895 & -1.9803 & -1.7068
    \end{pmatrix}
\end{align*}

Since the kernel is $1 \times 1$ and the channel of output is 3, the size of weight is $1 \times 1 \times 3 \times 3$, and size of bias is $1 \times 1 \times 3$.
